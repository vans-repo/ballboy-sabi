# NPFL clubs: history, rivalries and records

Background for Substack articles, X posts and the site's "big match" tags.
Researched 23 Nov 2026 from news reports and reference sites (sources at the
end). Where sources disagree, both figures are given; check before quoting.
The site's own numbers (table, results, form) come only from results entered
in admin, never from these pages.

## The 2026/27 league

20 clubs. Promoted for 2026/27: **Sporting Lagos, Inter Lagos, Doma United,
Ranchers Bees**. Relegated at the end of 2025/26: **Remo Stars** (the 2024/25
champions, down on the final day on goal difference, 17th on 47 points),
**El-Kanemi Warriors, Bayelsa United, Wikki Tourists**.

With Sporting Lagos and Inter Lagos up, Lagos has three top-flight clubs
(with Ikorodu City) for the first time since 1996: Lagos derbies are back.

**Defending champions: Enugu Rangers** (2025/26, 68 points).

## The giants

| Club | City | League titles | Continental |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Enugu Rangers** | Enugu | 9 (1974, 1975, 1977, 1981, 1982, 1984, 2016, 2023/24, 2025/26); joint record. Older sources say 7 or 8 | African Cup Winners' Cup 1977; CAF Champions Cup runners-up 1975 |
| **Enyimba** | Aba | 9 (2001, 2002, 2003, 2005, 2007, 2010, 2015, 2019, 2022/23); joint record. Some sources say 8 | **CAF Champions League 2003 and 2004**, the only Nigerian winners; CAF Super Cup |
| **Shooting Stars (3SC)** | Ibadan | (several in the 1970s–90s; count not confirmed) | African Cup Winners' Cup 1976; **CAF Cup 1992** (first winners); Champions Cup runners-up 1984 and 1996 |
| **Kano Pillars** | Kano | 4 (2008, 2012, 2013, 2014; 2013 and 2014 confirmed) | — |
| **Bendel Insurance** | Benin City | (older era) | **CAF Cup 1994** (beat Primeiro de Maio 3–1 on aggregate) |

Other champions in the current league: **Plateau United** (2017), **Rivers
United** (2021/22).

**Storylines worth knowing**
- Rangers and Enyimba are level on nine titles: whoever wins a tenth holds
  the record outright.
- Enyimba's 2003–04 side is often listed among Africa's greatest club teams.
- Kano Pillars' home record at the Sani Abacha Stadium: one compilation
  credits a 202-game unbeaten run at home (cited as a Guinness record);
  treat the figure with care.
- Highest scorer in league history (one compilation): Victor Ezeji, 148 goals.
- Longest unbeaten runs (one compilation, disputed): Akwa United 18 (2021),
  Bendel Insurance 18 (2023).
- Remo Stars joined the short list of defending champions relegated the
  season after their title.
- Ahmed Musa (former Super Eagles captain) won the Kano derby for Pillars
  in March 2026 (1–0 v Barau); Barau won the first top-flight Kano derby
  2–1 in October 2025.

## Derbies and rivalries

Confirmed by at least one news report (these drive the site's derby tags):

| Name | Clubs | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Oriental derby** | Enyimba v Enugu Rangers | The headline Nigerian rivalry. A 2024 meeting in Enugu was abandoned after a pitch invasion. Enyimba won 2–1 in Aba in late 2025. "Oriental derby" is also used for any two South-East clubs (Abia Warriors, Kun Khalifat too). |
| **Abia derby** | Abia Warriors v Enyimba | Same state (Umuahia v Aba); has seen crowd trouble in Umuahia. |
| **Kano derby** | Kano Pillars v Barau | New: Barau's first top-flight season was 2025/26. |
| **Lagos derby** | Ikorodu City, Inter Lagos, Sporting Lagos (any two) | Back for 2026/27, the first in about 10 years (last: MFM v Ikorodu City). |
| **North-West derby** | Kano Pillars v Katsina United | Described as "the border war on the A9 expressway" (Sai Masu Gida v the Changi Boys). One source. |
| **Rivalry** | Kano Pillars v Nasarawa United | Regional rivalry, one source. |

Not confirmed (owner to say yes or no before they get a tag): Bendel
Insurance v Warri Wolves (Mid-West), Plateau United v Nasarawa United
(North-Central), Kwara United v Shooting Stars, Niger Tornadoes v Kwara
United, Shooting Stars v the Lagos clubs (South-West).

Former derbies whose clubs aren't in the league this season: South-West derby
(Shooting Stars v Remo Stars, Shooting Stars v Sunshine Stars), North-Central
derby (Nasarawa United v Lobi Stars), Heartland v Enyimba/Ifeanyi Ubah.

## Hot matches this season

Worked out by the site from its own table each matchday (see
`web/src/lib/big-match.ts`), from matchday 5 onwards:
- **Top-two clash**: 1st v 2nd.
- **Top-of-table clash**: both in the top three.
- **Top v bottom**: a top-three side v a bottom-four side.
- **Relegation battle**: both in the bottom five.
