> 1. **Inter Lagos' points deduction is missing.** The site has Inter on **10 points**. > 2. **Rangers v Nasarawa United (Matchday 3) shows as "Abandoned".** Reports say heavy rain stopped it at 1–1 in the 63rd minute in Owerri on 17 September. They say it was finished the next morning and Rangers won **2–1** with a Daniel Itodo goal ([Daily Sports](https://dailysports.net/news/npfl-rangers-complete-comeback-win-over-nasarawa-united-after-rain-delayed-match/), [Football in Nigeria](https://www.footballinnigeria.com.ng/npfl/rangers-vs-nasarawa-united-suspended-due-to-heavy-rainfall-at-1-1/)). > 3. **Rivers United v Barau (Matchday 9) shows as "Abandoned".** Reports have Rivers on 22 points from 13 and Barau on 23 (3rd). That fits a **0–0** draw, and a Goal.com fixture list shows Rivers 0–0 Barau this season. I could not find a match report, so the result is unconfirmed.
>
> If all three are corrected, the top five would be **Rangers 28 (13 played), Pillars 25, Barau 23, Nasarawa 23, Rivers 22**. **Barau, not Nasarawa, would hold the CAF Confederation Cup place.** The brief below uses the site's numbers as they stand.

## 1. The week in one paragraph

Enugu Rangers beat Shooting Stars 2–0 in Awka, stretched their unbeaten run to eight league games and stayed top. Kano Pillars won 1–0 at Ikorodu City and are level with Rangers on 25 points, but Rangers have two games in hand. The goals came from Port Harcourt and Kano. Rivers United beat Niger Tornadoes 6–0, with a hat-trick from substitute Samson Olasupo. Barau beat bottom side Inter Lagos 5–0, and Joseph Atule scored the first hat-trick by a Barau player in the top flight. The shock of the round came in Benin. Doma United won 1–0 at Bendel Insurance, a result the Ballboy gave 8%, and it was Bendel's first home defeat of the season. In Aba, Abia Warriors and Enyimba drew 0–0 in a nervous derby, and both stay in the bottom four. Rangers go to Sporting Lagos on Wednesday for a game postponed from Matchday 10.

## 4. Storylines

### 1. Rangers' grip, and the shadow over it
**Evidence.** Rangers are top on 25 from 12 games, and the site has them unbeaten in eight league games, the longest current run in the league. They have two games in hand on Pillars. Press tables already show them three points clear on 28 from 13 ([Soccernet](https://soccernet.ng/2026/11/npfl-matchday-14-rivers-united-barau-hat-tricks-rangers-top.html), [QNC](https://qncnews.com/2026/11/22/emo-james-and-nwobodo-fire-rangers-past-3sc-2-to-0-as-flying-antelopes-stay-top-of-npfl/)). One of those wins is disputed. Bendel Insurance petitioned the NFF to change Rangers' 1–0 win on 4 November into a draw, because the goal came after the goalframe was displaced ([Soccernet](https://soccernet.ng/2026/11/npfl-bendel-insurance-petition-rangers-crossbar-goal-nff.html)). I found no ruling, so it is unconfirmed whether a decision has been made.
**Angle.** The defending champions are building a lead without fuss. If they win at Sporting on Wednesday and at Doma on Saturday, they would be three points clear even on the site's numbers. History is behind every point: Rangers and Enyimba are level on nine titles, and a tenth would give Rangers the record outright, back to back. What to watch: the petition. If it goes against Rangers they lose two points, and the club that brought it (Bendel) is in freefall. Rangers' 8-game run also faces two away trips, on Wednesday and Saturday.

### 2. Aba on the brink: two old powers in the bottom four
**Evidence.** Enyimba (18th) and Abia Warriors (19th) are both on 12 points from 14 games after a goalless Abia derby. Reports say Abia are now winless in eight league games ([QNC](https://qncnews.com/2026/11/23/enyimba-and-abia-warriors-share-goalless-draw-in-abia-derby-as-both-clubs-stay-in-the-drop-zone/)). The coaches' lines in the headlines: Enyimba's bench said "every draw in a derby is a success", while Abia coach Ladan Bosso said "Abia Warriors, Enyimba won't be relegated" (headline wording from [Complete Sports](https://www.completesports.com/euromatch-npfl-every-draw-in-a-derby-is-a-success-enyimba-coach-reacts-after-draw-against-abia-warriors/) and [Complete Sports](https://www.completesports.com/euromatch-npfl-abia-warriors-enyimba-wont-be-relegated-ladan-bosso/); I could not open the articles). Enyimba are the only Nigerian winners of the CAF Champions League (2003, 2004) and hold the joint-record nine league titles.
**Angle.** A point is called a success when you're 18th. Last season showed that no one is too big to go down: Remo Stars, champions the year before, were relegated on goal difference with 47 points. Enyimba have won once in their last five. Next up, Enyimba host Barau (4th), where the Ballboy gives them 49% to win, and Abia go to Pillars, where it gives them 8%. Kwara United (14) are in the bottom four too, and Kun Khalifat are only four points above the Aba pair. What to watch: if Enyimba drop points at home to Barau, the "too big to go down" question becomes the season's main story.

### 3. Six and five: Rivers United and Barau put on a show
**Evidence.** Rivers United (5th) have the best defence in the league, with 6 conceded in 12 games. With 22 goals scored, they also have the best goal difference (+16). Their 6–0 win ended a five-match winless run, and Olasupo scored his hat-trick off the bench. They have played twice fewer than most of the league and still have a home game against Sporting Lagos on 2 December. Off the pitch, Rivers are set to unveil a new front-of-shirt sponsor. The Complete Sports headline names it as FOS (unconfirmed). Eunisell has been on their shirt since 2016. Barau have lost once in 13 games (the site's form: D D W D W). Atule's hat-trick was a first for the club in the top flight, and some reports say it took them to 3rd.
**Angle.** The race for the Confederation Cup place (3rd) is the tightest in the league. Four clubs sit within two points, and two of them have games in hand. On goal difference, Rivers look like a title side that has drawn too often (six draws). If they turn draws into wins over the next fortnight, they're in the title race. Barau are in only their second top-flight season and are close to the CAF places. What to watch: Rivers' trip to Shooting Stars (a One To Watch, see below), and whether the Rivers v Barau abandoned game gets a result on the site.

### 4. Bendel Insurance: from 3rd to 6th, at war with the league
**Evidence.** Bendel lost 0–1 at home to Doma, their first home defeat of the season and their second loss in a row after 1–2 at Shooting Stars. They have lost three of their last five. Doma coach Deji Ayeni admitted his team were "lucky" (headline: "We Were Lucky To Beat Bendel Insurance In Benin", [Complete Sports](https://www.completesports.com/euromatch-npfl-we-were-lucky-to-beat-bendel-insurance-in-benin-doma-united-coach-ayeni/)). A Blueprint headline says Doma players got N4 million for the win (unconfirmed, headline only). Bendel's record is strange: 7 wins, 0 draws, 7 losses, the only club in the league without a draw. Their chairman has reportedly threatened to pull the club out of the league if Rangers' disputed goal stands (ThisDay, via search summary; unconfirmed).
**Angle.** Bendel are all or nothing: no draws in 14 games. A team that only wins or loses is hard to read, and that's why the Ballboy gives the bottom side a real chance of an upset against them this weekend. What to watch: Saturday's trip to Inter Lagos (the Match of the Week), and whether the petition story escalates.

### 5. (Short) Inter Lagos: the bottom side and the missing three points
**Evidence.** Inter lost 5–0 at Barau and are bottom on 10 points on the site (7 in reports, after the October deduction). They have conceded 25 goals, the most in the league. They have only won twice, but one of those was their first-ever top-flight home win, 2–1 over Abia Warriors on 15 November ([Soccernet](https://soccernet.ng/2026/11/npfl-inter-lagos-first-home-win-abia-warriors-onikan.html)).
**Angle.** One of the new Lagos clubs is learning how hard the top flight is. They are six points from safety on the site's numbers, and nine if the deduction is counted. Saturday at home to Bendel is a must-win.
